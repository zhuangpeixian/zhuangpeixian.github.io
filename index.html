Peixian Zhuang (庄培显)

I am IEEE Senior Member, CCF Senior Member, CAAI Member. Currently, I am an Associate Professor in University of Science and Technology Beijing. From 2020 to 2022, I was the Postdoctoral Fellow from the Department of Automation, Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Qionghai Dai. In 2016, I received Ph.D. degree from the School of Information Science and Engineering, Xiamen University, advised by Prof. Xinghao Ding. My research interests focus on underwater image processing, non-parameteric Bayesian, sparse representation, and deep learning.
